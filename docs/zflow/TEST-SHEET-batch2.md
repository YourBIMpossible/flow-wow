# Z-Flow batch 2: test sheet

Draft by Twin, DISPATCH section 580 item 4. About 10 minutes, in walking order. Nothing below is marked passed; tick each box yourself.

Settings open from a left-click on the minimap button (the small round Z-Flow button). The Flow button (the big rotation button) casts; it never opens settings.

| Step | Do this | You should see | Wrong looks like | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (R1) | Log in. Look under the Flow button. | Two buttons. The self buff button sits under the Flow button; with nothing to cast it is dark with the word "Buffs" under it. Point at it: the tooltip says why it is empty. | Only one button, a blank tooltip, or a lua error. | [ ] |
| 2 (R8) | Look at the "Your buffs" and "Advice" panels. | Each shows one line saying what it is. "Your buffs" has a close button. Click it: the strip goes away and a chat line says how to bring it back. | No one-line text, no close button, or no chat line. | [ ] |
| 3 (R2) | Out of a fight, with Mining or Herbalism known and no tracking on, look at the self button. | It offers Find Minerals (or Find Herbs). Click it: tracking turns on. Nothing casts by itself. | Tracking turns on without a click, or it is offered while mounted or in a fight. | [ ] |
| 4 (R3) | Left-click the minimap button, Combat and Class, set "Your role". Hover a gear item in your bags. | The bag arrow and the item tooltip give the same verdict for that role. | The tooltip and the bag disagree, or the advice flips after equipping the suggested item. | [ ] |
| 5 (R4) | Open your bags (Bagnon or BagBrother if installed, else the normal bags). | Bag marks and badge letters show on gear slots. Another character's bags in Bagnon show no marks. | No marks in Bagnon, marks on another character's bags, or a lua error. | [ ] |
| 6 (R6) | With quests in your log, press M. | The quest route is drawn on the world map. Take or finish a quest: it updates. | No route, or a route that does not change. | [ ] |
| 7 (R7) | Set a map pin. | The arrow is green at any distance; the arrow, its purple echo and the map route agree on colour. | A pin arrow that turns red or yellow with distance. | [ ] |
| 8 (R5) | Open a profession step, if one shows. | It names a trainer, a vendor or a known zone, and the arrow points there (with Questie it can take a few seconds). With no known place it says so. | A made-up place, or the arrow never appears out of a fight. | [ ] |
| 9 (R9) | Right-click the quest arrow, then Show, Flow windows, Inventory. Then right-click the arrow, Dungeon quests, pick a dungeon, Plan the run. | The inventory list opens; the dungeon "Get ready" stops open. No typing. | A menu row missing, or a window that does not open. | [ ] |
| 10 (all) | Turn one item off: left-click the minimap button, search "Show the self buff button", untick it. | The self button goes away. Tick it again: it comes back. | The setting is not found, or the button stays. | [ ] |

If anything is wrong: close the game, put back the kept ZFlow copy (see the install note), start the game.
